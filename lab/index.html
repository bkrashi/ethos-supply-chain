ETHOS · Ethical decision-making laboratory

Every battery has a story.

Behind a phone battery is a chain of mines, refiners, factories and ports, and the people who live and work along it. In this lab, six AI agents with competing objectives debate how one electronics company should source its batteries.

Real evidenceFacts come from the ILO and U.S. Department of Labor, at country and sector level.
